FoodLink

FoodLink is a zero-cost community platform built to combat food waste and foster direct sharing. Whether you are a restaurant owner with unsold daily specials, an event planner with untouched catering trays, or a host with party surplus, FoodLink gives your food a second life. List in Seconds: Food donors post details, pickup locations, and collection windows for available surplus. Claim for Free: Nearby individuals, community organizers, or shelters reserve portions directly through the platform. Collect & Divert Waste: Food is picked up at designated times—completely free of cost—diverting quality meals away from landfills and straight to those who can enjoy them. By making local food sharing immediate, transparent, and accessible, FoodLink empowers communities to fight hunger, cut carbon footprints, and eliminate unnecessary food waste together.
